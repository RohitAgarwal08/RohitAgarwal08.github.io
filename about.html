About Me

I am a dedicated and passionate Ph.D. Research Scholar at the National Institute of Technology Durgapur, specializing in Computer Science and Engineering. My research focuses on the design of Machine Learning-based CAD Systems for Medical Image Analysis. I hold an M.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from the University Institute of Technology, Burdwan, and a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from the Academy of Technology, Kolkata.

During my academic journey, I have also gained experience as a Teaching Assistant at the National Institute of Technology Durgapur and as an Assistant Teacher (Computer) at Shemford Futuristic School, Burdwan.
